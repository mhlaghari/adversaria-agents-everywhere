Adversaria is a privacy-first, bot-free meeting notetaker for macOS and Windows.
It records meeting audio on-device with dual-channel capture: system audio ("Them") plus the microphone ("Me").
Transcription runs locally with Whisper (faster-whisper on Windows, MLX Whisper on Apple Silicon).
Structured notes are generated by a local LLM through Ollama; nothing leaves the machine.
Meeting history, notes, and artifacts are stored in a local SQLite database.
Workspaces group meetings and attached files and give agents grounded context for tasks.
Live Copilot listens during a meeting, catches questions, and detects commitments for approval.
Recordings are deleted once a meeting is transcribed; a failed transcription is kept on-device until retried.
